Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who is responsible

Responsible for this site and the data processed through it: Juan Berliner, official tour guide, Berlin, Germany. For any privacy question you can message me on WhatsApp at +49 157 3621 4462.

Booking form

If you send the booking form, I receive your name, your email, the tour and date you choose, the number of people and, if you enter them, your phone number and message. I use this data only to answer your request and organise the tour (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). It is sent through Web3Forms, which forwards it to me by email. I keep it for as long as needed to handle your request and meet my legal obligations.

WhatsApp

The WhatsApp buttons open that app with a pre-written message. From then on, your conversation is also subject to WhatsApp's privacy policy.

Hosting and analytics

This site is hosted on Vercel, which logs technical data for each visit, such as the IP address, to serve the pages and keep them secure. I also use Vercel Web Analytics to see in aggregate which pages are visited; this service does not use cookies. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in maintaining and improving the site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Third-party content

Some pages load resources from other services, and your browser sends them your IP address so they can be displayed: Google Fonts (typefaces), Tailwind CDN and unpkg (page code), Google Maps (meeting point maps), OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap (recommendations map) and other websites that host some of the photos. Google Maps may use its own cookies. Some of these providers may process data outside the European Union.

Your rights

You can ask me to access, correct or delete your data, restrict its use, object to it and receive it in a portable format. You can also complain to a data protection authority; in Berlin this is the Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit.
