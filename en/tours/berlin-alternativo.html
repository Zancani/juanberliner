About this tour

Berlin is the world capital of alternative culture, street art, techno, freedom and libertinism. Why? Since when? Let's go beyond the traditional monuments to discover the real Berlin hidden in the city's bohemian neighborhoods.

We'll meet by the Tränenpalast to walk through Oranienburger Straße, the art nouveau courtyards of Hackesche Höfe, the multicultural neighborhood of Kotti, and vibrant Kreuzberg. The tour ends before the more than 100 murals that make up the East Side Gallery.

Note: We'll use public transport for transfers (Zones AB). A valid transport pass is required.

Frequently asked questions

What kind of places do we visit on the alternative tour?

The tour explores neighborhoods like Kreuzberg and Friedrichshain, visiting street art, alternative cultural centers, historic squats, independent galleries, urban markets, and spaces that reflect Berlin's counterculture.

Is this a street art tour?

Street art is an important part of the tour, but not the only thing. We also explore the history of Berlin's alternative scene, club culture, the squatting movement, urban transformation, and the creativity that has defined Berlin since the fall of the Wall.

How much does the alternative tour cost?

The tour is priced at €18 per person. It includes 3 hours and 45 minutes of guided walking through Berlin's most creative neighborhoods with an official guide who knows the local scene in depth.

Which areas of Berlin are covered?

The tour focuses on Kreuzberg (Berlin's quintessential alternative district), Friedrichshain (underground scene and street art), and parts of the east where Berlin's alternative culture reaches its fullest expression.

How is it different from traditional tourist tours?

Unlike traditional tours that focus on monuments and major landmarks, this tour takes you to the authentic Berlin that locals experience: hidden courtyards, artist workshops, murals, alternative cafés, and the vibrant underground culture that makes the city unique.
